## Current operational limits

QQ still needs the chosen local runtime and a valid QQ login. The app now offers
the separately managed Node route as well as the existing QQ/Shell route; its
pre-authentication QR lifecycle does not establish account login. Upstream
credential invalidation can still require a fresh login. The application
distinguishes explicit invalidation from network reconnects but cannot prevent
QQ from expiring a session. Local voice models require the separate
[pinned setup](VOICE_LOCAL_SETUP.md); they are not bundled or downloaded
automatically. The computer must remain on for locally hosted accounts and voice.
Plain WeChat friend accounts, Moments and unrestricted group participation are
not implemented.
