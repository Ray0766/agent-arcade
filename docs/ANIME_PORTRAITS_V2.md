## Direction and references

The prior restrained anime-helper portraits and a richer anime-game character direction were the two concrete alternatives. The owner selected the latter after viewing Bothouse's actual orange/dragon/whale/cat assets. Two specific observations informed the revision: the orange portrait uses a flower/ribbon silhouette and crisp shading to identify its character; the dragon portrait combines horns, luminous eyes and ornate clothes for an expressive fantasy identity. The whale adds ocean accessories and a frilled costume, while the cat deliberately keeps a more chibi face and star motif. We adapt these broad devices with original faces and costumes. We do not reproduce the inspected images, import their licenses, or adopt a decorative background that obscures small avatars.

All five use local transparent PNGs, fine linework, layered cel shading and readable color families. The existing light chat room, typography and permission/status behavior remain unchanged. CSS contain and modest padding protect hair ornaments inside circle masks. Better recognition is an art-direction hypothesis, not a measured usability gain. This revision adds no provider integration or runtime dependency.
